Yoshiyuki Kobayashi, Ph.D.

Lecturer, Laboratory of Medical Data Science, Faculty of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences, Hoshi University, Tokyo, Japan

Research

My field is informatics in the pharmaceutical sciences. I collect and analyze the data that governments and companies rely on when they make decisions about chemicals in medicines, pesticides and food. Current topics:

  • Machine-learning QSAR/QSPR models for the environmental fate of pesticides and other chemicals
  • Exposure assessment of food allergens, and international comparison of regulations for health foods and biostimulants
  • Large language model-assisted text mining (LATeM) of research trends
  • Drug development, orphan drug lag and digital health, with members of our laboratory

Before moving to academia, I spent more than ten years in the pharmaceutical, agrochemical and consulting industries, including pesticide registration for overseas markets.

JSPS KAKENHI Grant-in-Aid for Early-Career Scientists (25K18320), FY2025–FY2026, principal investigator. The project compares biostimulant regulations across countries and examines how they affect innovation and market development.
